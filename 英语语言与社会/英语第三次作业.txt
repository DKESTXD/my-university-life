The social and regional accent variation in Britain presents a pyramid structure.The
horizontal axis of the figure can represent the regional variation and the vertical axis 
can represent the social variation.The bottom edge of the pyramid,which means the
lowest class,has the greatest regional variation and the most localized accent.With
the increase in social class ,the accent has less and less regional variation.The apex of
the pyramid show that the highest class has no regional variation due to the special
status of RP.RP is not an accent of a certain region, it is a separate accent that is used 
exclusively by the upper class or the public media.The higher your social class is, the 
closer your accent is to the RP, resulting in lower regional variation.This negative 
correlation between social class and the localization of accents leads to the emergence 
of social-class accents, which created barriers to the blending of different classes and accents
